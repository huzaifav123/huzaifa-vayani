Four leaks I look for in a fashion store

Tracking gaps

Pixel and CAPI setup, duplicate or missing purchase events, and orders closed in WhatsApp or Instagram DMs that Meta never sees.

RTO and exchanges, counted separately

Undelivered parcels and size or fit exchanges are different problems. I split them by collection, size, city and courier.

Sale-period margin

Contribution margin per campaign after discounts and returns, so a sale that “worked” does not quietly lose money.

Spend on sold-out stock

Ads still running on sizes or designs you no longer have. Catalog ads with inventory sync help stop this.
