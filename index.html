Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee sales or a return on ad spend?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee that. I control campaign setup, testing and optimisation. Sales also depend on your product, price, offer, website and how enquiries are handled.

What is a WhatsApp conversation in your reporting?

It is a person who started a chat with your business after seeing an ad. It is a lead, not a sale, and I report it that way.

Do you work with Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. Website advertising is a service I am expanding. I will be clear about what I can do now and what needs extra tracking work.

Is the ad budget included in your fees?

No. You pay the advertising budget to Meta directly. My fees are separate.

Do you create the ad creatives?

Creative production is not automatically included. I advise on creative testing, and we can agree scope if you want more.

What is the difference between the discovery call and the audit?

The 15-minute call is free and is a conversation. The audit costs Rs 5,000 and gives you a written review of your account.

Do you work as an agency?

No. I am an independent marketer, and you work with me directly.
